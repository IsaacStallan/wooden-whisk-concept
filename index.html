Our place

A neighbourhood café on Albany Street

From the first flat white at 6:30am to a relaxed lunch, The Wooden Whisk is St Leonards’ local for good coffee, generous plates and a warm welcome.

Coffee from Single Origin Roasters

Our beans come from Single Origin Roasters in Surry Hills, one of Sydney’s pioneering specialty roasters. We pull them as espresso, brew them slow by hand as pour-over, and drip them overnight for cold brew served over ice.
